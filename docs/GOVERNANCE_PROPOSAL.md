# Experimental governance proposal

This proposal belongs to the fork. It does not change current official PerkCommons governance.

## Roles and accountability

Publish role counts and responsibilities; publish individual names only with consent. Moderators disclose provider, applicant, sponsor, financial and close-person conflicts and recuse when material. Administrators manage publication/infrastructure but cannot bypass evidence or second-review rules.

Require two distinct reviewers for sensitive removals, sponsorship/featured decisions, high-impact disputes, appeals of permanent removal, policy exceptions and material conflicts. The first reviewer cannot satisfy the second review. Emergency scam/malware/privacy suppression may happen immediately, followed by independent review within a documented window.

## Appeals and corrections

Providers and community members may submit evidence-backed corrections without payment. Appeals receive a public-safe tracking reference, decision reason, reviewer separation and response target. Git history and tombstones preserve decisions without exposing private reports or moderator notes.

## Sponsorship and editorial independence

Sponsorship never purchases verification, status or default-search eligibility. Sponsored/featured records disclose type, reason and expiry. Conflicted decision-makers recuse; material sponsorship decisions require two reviewers and appear in transparency reporting.

## Ownership and succession

Maintain a private infrastructure-access register, at least two recoverable owners for critical systems, documented emergency rotation, annual access review and a succession process. Removing an owner must not erase public data, audit state or recovery capability.

## Decisions and quorum

Routine editorial decisions follow published policy. Policy changes, sponsorship standards, permanent removals on contested grounds, infrastructure ownership and license changes require documented proposal, conflict declarations, a quorum defined by the active roster and recorded votes/abstentions. Security emergencies permit temporary action with retrospective review.

## Transparency

Publish periodic aggregate counts for submissions, decisions/reasons, corrections, appeals, sponsorship, removals, retention and material incidents. Do not publish identities, contact details, raw reports, small-cell sensitive statistics or security details that enable abuse.

## Software enforcement

The target database stores assignment, revision, first/second reviewer, conflict and appeal state. Security-definer RPCs reject stale revision and same-reviewer second approval. UI affordances are not sufficient enforcement. Policy versions and compatibility metadata accompany publication batches.
